## What is the purpose of this homework assignment

You are going to spend this semester learning how to create software using AI. AI writes code faster than you can read it. While speed and lowering barriers to developing software is the whole appeal, there are down sided. When a tool can generate three hundred lines in twenty seconds, the question stops being "can I produce this?" and becomes "can I tell what just changed, can I undo it, and can somebody else check it before it reaches a customer?" Version control is the answer to all three. Version control provide a foundation for moving fast safely. 

Version control is foundational to Agentic software development.

## Your GitHub account

**If you do not have a GitHub account,** create one at https://github.com/join
- If you do not create your GitHub account with your U of I email, follow the steps below to add your U of I address.

**If you already have a GitHub account, do not create a second one.** 
- Add your U of I email to the account you already have. You will keep your existing history, and you will not spend the semester switching accounts.

### Adding your U of I email

1. Sign in to GitHub with your existing account.
2. Go to **https://github.com/settings/emails**
   (or: click your profile picture in the upper right, choose **Settings**, then **Emails** in the left sidebar)
3. Under **Add email address**, type your U of I address and click **Add**.
4. GitHub sends you a verification email. Open it and click the link inside. **Check your junk folder** if it does not arrive within a few minutes.
5. Once verified, the address appears in your list with no warning label next to it.

That is all that is required. You do not have to make it your primary address.

**Optional:** if you would rather have GitHub notifications go to your U of I inbox, select it from the **Primary email address** dropdown on the same page.

**Check the commit email privacy setting.**

- I have this setting enabled. It's up to you.
- If you want your email address to be private, on the same page, under Primary email address, make sure "Keep my email addresses private" is checked, and "Block command line pushes that expose my email" just below it. New accounts (should) have both on by default. If you have an older account, confirm it. This keeps your personal email out of public commit records for the rest of the semester.


The default keeps your personal email address out of public commit records, and it is the right default.

### If you get "email is already in use"

This error means the address is already attached to a different GitHub account, which usually means you created one earlier and forgot. You cannot have the same email on two accounts.

1. Sign out, then sign in to the other account. Use the password reset link on your U of I address if you do not remember the password.
2. Decide which account you want to keep. Consider keeping the one with work in it. If you are not sure, we can sort it out after class.
3. On the account you are **not** keeping, go to https://github.com/settings/emails and delete the U of I address.
4. Sign back into the account you are keeping and add the address there.

**If you are unsure, we can sort it out after the next class.**  
**GitHub cannot merge two accounts. If both have work you care about, bring that to class and we will sort it out.**
